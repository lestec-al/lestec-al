Effective date: May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the free mobile Android application “Eventify” (“App”) published by ALIAKSEI YURHEL.

This page informs you of my policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use my App.

### Information Collection and Use
Eventify does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information to me or any third party.

All data you enter into the App — is stored locally on your device using an SQLite database managed by the App.

The App does not use any analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or third-party SDKs.

Since all data remains on your device, you control its security. However, please note that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security of data stored locally against device compromise.

### Import/Export Feature
The App allows you to manually import or export your data to/from your device’s local storage. You are solely responsible for any files you export or share. The App does not automatically access, upload, or transmit these files elsewhere.

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after posting.
